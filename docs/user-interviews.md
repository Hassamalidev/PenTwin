# User interviews: willingness to pay (Task 0.6)

**Goal:** talk to 10 students before fixing prices. **Gate:** revisit the price tiers once all 10 are filled in.

## How to run it

- 10 minutes each, in person or on a call. Don't pitch first: ask questions 1-3 before describing PenTwin.
- Ask about what they did last time, not what they would do. Past behaviour is more reliable than predictions.
- For question 4, show a sample output page and let them name a number before you mention any price.
- Write answers down in their words.

## Script

1. **Current solution.** "Think of the last time you had to hand in something handwritten. How did you get it done, and how long did it take?"
2. **Pain.** "What was the most annoying part of that? How often does it come up in a month?"
3. **Alternatives tried.** "Have you ever used an app, a font, or paid someone to do it? What did it cost and what was wrong with it?"
4. **Price tolerance.** (Show the sample.) "If this were in your own handwriting, what would you expect to pay per month? At what price is it too expensive to consider? At what price would it seem too cheap to trust?"
5. **Must-haves.** "What would it have to do for you to use it every time? What would make you stop using it?"
